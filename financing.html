Pre-qualify online with our financing partner, Acorn Finance, and compare payment options from multiple lenders before you start your remodel.

How it works

  1. Pre-qualify online. Click the Acorn Finance banner to open their pre-qualification page in a new tab.
  2. Compare your options. Acorn Finance shows payment options from multiple lenders.
  3. Talk to us. Bring your questions to your free estimate and we will help you plan the project around your budget.

Financing is provided by Acorn Finance and is subject to approval. ARKA Remodeling does not make lending decisions.
